# Neelaya Brand Color Palette

**Brand:** Neelaya Freediving & Mermaid Academy  
**Signature:** *From sky to sea, your home in the blue.*

This palette is derived directly from the Neelaya logo’s sky-to-ocean gradient. It moves from airy sky blues down through surface ocean tones into deep, immersive navy, with warm accents inspired by coral reefs and sunlit sand.

## Recommended Background Pairings

- **Airy hero section:** Sky 50 background + Ocean 500 text + Ocean 400 accents
- **Deep ocean section:** Deep 900 background + Sky 50 text + Sky 200 highlights
- **Sunlit beach section:** Sand background + Deep 900 text + Coral accents
- **Coral reef accent card:** Deep 800 background + Sky 50 text + Coral CTA
- **Gradient hero:** `linear-gradient(180deg, #E8F4FA 0%, #9AD4F0 40%, #1E7EAE 100%)`

---

## Usage Notes

- Use **Sky 50–Sky 200** for light, breathable spaces that evoke air and surface water.
- Use **Ocean 400–Ocean 600** for interactive elements, primary branding, and focal points.
- Use **Deep 700–Deep 900** for depth, footer areas, dark mode, and high-contrast text backgrounds.
- Use **Coral** sparingly as a warm accent to draw attention without breaking the blue narrative.
- Use **Sand** and **Seafoam** as secondary accents for earthy, tropical, or success contexts.
